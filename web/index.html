01

The first time

required

The first time, your mod build must be installed with Make Noise's official audio update procedure, exactly like an official firmware update.

The SD-card updater is itself one of the mods, so it isn't on your module until this first audio install. After that, updates come from the card.

  1. 1

    Build below and download the update WAV.

  2. 2

    Remove the SD card. Connect your computer or phone to Left Signal IN, at about 80% volume.

  3. 3

    Power on while holding REC. Only the SPLICE window lights, in green. Release REC.

  4. 4

    Play the update WAV from start to end. The windows flash while it loads, then all lights flash when it's done.

    Don't touch anything while it plays. If it fails, power off and start again from step 3.
  5. 5

    Finish as Make Noise's instructions say, including the Varispeed calibration: power on holding SPLICE, turn Varispeed fully both ways, press SPLICE, then power-cycle.

  6. 6

    From then on, put mgmod.bin at the root of the card and power on. When the update has worked, the file is renamed mgmod.ok.
